Playing together

  • Knotted! is online co-op for 2 to 4 players. Every player needs their own device and an internet connection.
  • To invite someone, tap + next to Friends, then share or copy the party code. The other player opens Join a party, enters the code and taps Join.
  • With no party open, tapping Play searches for a partner with Quick Match.

Purchases and diamonds

  • Diamonds are used only for cosmetic items. You can buy them in the game or earn them with optional rewarded ads.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page or Google Play's order history.
